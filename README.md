# Hypotheses
H1: Higher ACE exposure will be associated with whole-brain structural MRI differences, measured using voxel-based morphometry-derived gray matter volume. 

H2: These ACE-related structural differences will be most pronounced in canonical stress- and emotion-regulation regions, including the hippocampus, amygdala, medial prefrontal cortex, anterior cingulate cortex, orbitofrontal cortex, and insula. 

# Research Questions
1. Is higher ACE exposure associated with whole-brain structural MRI differences in gray matter volume?
   
2. Are ACE-related gray matter volume differences most pronounced in stress- and emotion-regulation regions, including the hippocampus, amygdala, medial prefrontal cortex, anterior cingulate cortex, orbitofrontal cortex, and insula?
